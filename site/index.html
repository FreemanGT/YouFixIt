Plain answers.

Does it cost anything?

No, and it never will. The code is on GitHub under the MIT License, so you can read every line before you install it.

Why isn’t it on the Mac App Store?

It has to see other programs to help with them, so it can’t be sandboxed. You get it here or on GitHub.

How do I install it, and get new versions?

macOS 14.4 or newer, on Apple silicon and Intel. Open the disk image and drag YouFixIt to Applications; its face shows up in your menu bar. There’s no automatic updater: the download here always points to the newest release.

What if it closes something I need?

Press Undo within a minute and the apps it closed come back. Anything it cleared from disk is in the Trash, so you can put it back from there.

Does it use AI?

No. It runs plain rules on your Mac.

Does anything leave my Mac?

Not from the app: no network, no account, no analytics. Ask Claude is the one thing that does, and only when you press it. It opens a new Claude chat with the question written, your home folder shown as ~ and anything like a password blanked out. Claude gets the words, never your Mac.

What happens to my email?

It goes to a Google Sheet the maker reads, along with the page you came from and a short note of your platform. It’s used to say hello and share news about YouFixIt, rarely. Reply to any email to be removed.
