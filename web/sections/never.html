When in doubt, it stays.

Every suggestion is checked twice: when it’s found, and again the moment you press the button.

  • Never force-quits. Apps quit normally, so they can ask to save.

  • Never deletes on its own. Files go to the Trash.

  • Never stops what’s busy on its own. Busy apps, just-written folders and macOS itself stay.

  • Never closes your tools. Terminals, editors, browsers, messaging, password managers, VPNs and sync apps stay open.
